Ask about a count

Based in Atlanta. Working with tree growers in Georgia.

What you get

After one pass through your field, you get three things.

A count you can check

We count the trees in each block and each row. You get the total, and you get the number by species, or by height class.

A map on your phone

Open a link on your phone and see your own field. Tap a block to see what is in it. No app to install and no training needed.

A record for every tree

Every tree gets its own ID and its own line in a spreadsheet, so your office and your sales team work from the same numbers.

No more counting by hand.

How it works

1. We walk your farm with you

We come out once, look at your blocks, and agree on what to count and when.

2. We collect it

We cover your rows the way that suits the field, with a drone from the air or on foot. We stay out of your way and we do not need your crew.

3. You get your map

We send you a link and a spreadsheet. If the numbers do not look right to you, tell us and we go back out.

Talk to us

Tell us how many acres you have and what you grow, and we will tell you straight whether we can help you this season.
